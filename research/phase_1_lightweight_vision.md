# Phase 1 — Lightweight vision (2024–2026)
Survey compact deep-learning vision broadly, not OCR only. Study size/accuracy/latency trade-offs near 1M params and transfer to text crops. Discover via Semantic Scholar; verify primary papers, proceedings, official repos. Record query/date/IDs, screening rationale, task, data/split, params, FLOPs, resolution, hardware/latency, metrics, code, limitations, evidence locator. Mark unknowns; avoid comparing incompatible setups. Separate author-reported facts and synthesis. Exploratory only; never tune on Phase 2 holdout. An initial, non-systematic Semantic Scholar discovery/screening pass and primary-source checks are documented in `phase_1_semantic_scholar_screened.md`; architecture selection and comprehensive screening remain open.
